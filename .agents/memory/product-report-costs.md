---
name: Product report cost basis
description: The inventory product report uses weighted inbound unit costs and separates them from current product cost.
---

The product report's weighted average cost is calculated from purchase and return quantities multiplied by their recorded unit costs. Current inventory value remains quantity on hand multiplied by the product's current cost.

Inventory journal entries use the movement's recorded unit cost; only movements without an explicit cost fall back to the product's current cost.

**Why:** Purchase costs can change over time, so one current product cost cannot explain the historical cost of all inbound stock or the accounting value of a recorded movement.

**How to apply:** Keep purchase and return movement unit costs in the weighted average and journal entries; use current product cost only for current valuation or as a fallback for legacy movements with no recorded cost.
