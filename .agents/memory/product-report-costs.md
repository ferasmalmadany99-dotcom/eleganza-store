---
name: Product report cost basis
description: The inventory product report uses weighted inbound unit costs and separates them from current product cost.
---

The product report's weighted average cost is calculated from purchase and return quantities multiplied by their recorded unit costs. Current inventory value remains quantity on hand multiplied by the product's current cost.

**Why:** Purchase costs can change over time, so one current product cost cannot explain the historical cost of all inbound stock.

**How to apply:** Keep purchase and return movement unit costs in the weighted average; use the current product cost for the current valuation shown in the product record.
